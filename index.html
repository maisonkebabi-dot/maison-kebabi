The Art of the Persian Grill

Charcoal Smoke & Saffron Alchemy

Every skewer is hand-forged by weight, steeped in wild Persian Sargol saffron, and seared over glowing hardwood embers—never gas or electric coils.

I

Live Hardwood Embers

Pure radiant heat from dense hardwood charcoal imparts an unmistakable smoky Persian hearth aroma while locking in natural juices within seconds.

II

Sargol Saffron Glaze

Hand-harvested crimson saffron threads bloomed over cracked ice and emulsified with cultured butter and fresh lemon—basted continuously over the fire.

III

Hand-Pressed Koobideh

Prime minced cuts kneaded with pressed sweet onions and rock salt, ribbed deftly by thumb along wide 25 mm stainless steel blades.

Heraldic Symbolism

Anatomy of Our Emblem

The Maison Kebabi crest weaves classical Persian illumination geometry with the tools of the master pitmaster—honoring live charcoal, forged steel skewers, and royal hospitality.

01. Persian Cartouche & Shield

The ornate central cartouche reflects traditional Persian manuscript borders and our uncompromising standards of open-flame craft.

02. Interlaced MK Monogram

Golden flourishes frame the central MK letters, uniting Parisian atelier precision with timeless Iranian culinary heritage.

03. Crossed Steel Skewers

Flanking flat skewers laden with charred meats and vine tomatoes evoke the bustling grill hearths of Tehran and Isfahan.

04. Grillade & Feu Seal

Crowned with crisp typography honoring fire, smoke, and gustatory harmony at every table we serve.

The Botanical Counterpoint

The Art of Doozi Refreshment

In traditional Persian feasts, rich charcoal kebabs are balanced by cooling herbal elixirs. Our signature Doozi preparations cleanse the palate and elevate every bite.

Whipped organic yogurt infused with crushed Persian wild mint (نعناع فلفلی), damask rose petals, and chilled sparkling spring water, paired with 7-year aged pickled garlic (Sir-e Torshi).

Wild Mountain Mint

Hand-rubbed highland mint leaves for aromatic cooling and effortless digestion.
