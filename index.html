HERALDIC ARCHITECTURE

Anatomy of Our Emblem

The Maison Kebabi visual identity is an intricate architectural harmony reflecting centuries of Persian grilling traditions, live charcoal embers, and artisanal butchery.

01. Shield & Crest Geometry

The ornate central cartouche and heraldic shield represent the uncompromising standards of our master grillers and time-honored smoke recipes.

02. Interlaced MK Monogram

Clean typography and golden flourishes frame the central MK letters, symbolizing refined luxury and authentic culinary heritage.

03. Flanking Skewer Cross

Crossed stainless steel skewers loaded with aromatic kebabs and charred vegetables evoke the bustling open-flame hearths of ancient Persian bazaars.

04. Grillade & Feu Inscription

Finished with crisp typography rendering MAISON KEBABI, GUSTATORY ACCENTS, and GRILLADE & FEU in sovereign equilibrium.
